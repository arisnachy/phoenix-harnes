# PHOENIX — community launch kit

Official repository: https://github.com/arisnachy/phoenix-harnes

## Short description

PHOENIX is an open-source, provider-agnostic AI agent workspace for supported local and cloud models, built around Kira, tools and continuity. It is under active development.

## English announcement (draft for authorized channels)

I'm building PHOENIX, an open-source AI agent workspace from the Dominican Republic. The idea is simple: your workspace should not depend on a single AI model or provider. We're building a common environment for supported local and cloud models, tools and agent workflows. Early testers and contributors are welcome. If you try it, tell us what works and what breaks.

https://github.com/arisnachy/phoenix-harnes

## Launch checklist

- Verify a clean Windows installation and the current release; show the build/version used in every demo.
- Record a short authentic demo with a local model, provider selection and a completed task.
- Publish through the maintainer's authorized accounts: GitHub, LinkedIn, Hacker News (Show HN), relevant Reddit communities and Product Hunt when release quality is appropriate.
- Follow community rules, disclose affiliation, invite feedback, and avoid mass unsolicited promotion or purchased engagement.
- Track baseline stars, forks, release downloads, issues and independently reported successful installs weekly. Downloads are not unique users; stars are not active installs.
- Preserve upstream attribution and describe experimental features accurately. Do not market clinical features as validated or certified without evidence.
