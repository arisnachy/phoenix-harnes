# Agent Note: APEX domain-specific research and professional delivery

Status: implemented

English | [中文](2026-11-06-apex-expert-research.zh.md)

## Problem

The governed PHOENIX investigation returned actual excerpts and source receipts, but offered no structured professional deliverable requirements and no opt-in broader evidence acquisition tier.

## Decision

Add a deterministic expert research contract tied to the existing domain router and retrieval receipts. It records specialty-specific technical dimensions, limitations, image and chart restrictions, and professional answer structure. The optional expert acquisition depth increases bounded reading while preserving standard and deep budgets. HARDNESS guides final delivery across all mission types.

## Alternatives considered

**New independent agent:** Rejected because it would duplicate HARDNESS lifecycle state, tool authority, and cost.

**Unlimited research:** Rejected because provider latency, quotas and blocked sites would make ordinary tasks unreliable.

**Mandatory generated images:** Rejected because provenance and retrieval speed are not guaranteed; use authentic visuals only where useful.

## Consequences

Professional guidance now follows evidence metadata rather than a generic claim of expertise. Two readable owners still do not prove every assertion. The longer expert tier is opt-in and capped; full-text scientific synthesis, guaranteed fast external image rendering, and independent human-expert superiority benchmarks remain outside this slice.

## Verification

Focused tests cover discipline routing, clinical dosage context, source independence and visual rules. Typecheck, repository CI, Windows regressions and real end-to-end quality measurements remain required before production claims.
