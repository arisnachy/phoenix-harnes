# Universal operational progress fence

## Root cause
The Goal driver measured only completion-gate progress, so a long sequence of assistant-only or identical tool attempts showed zero durable change but continued through unlimited execution-window revisions. A new Sol strategy or repeated specialist inspection was treated as forward motion even without new evidence. Large HTML publication exposed the same loop when no direct file-input contract existed.

## Resolution
Track real goal-work receipts across revisions and restarts: distinctive successful tool invocations (stable tool+argument identity, not call ID), newly completed checklist or team items, and independently verified completion criteria. A narrated plan, fresh turn or identical re-read does not count as progress. After two stagnant rounds favor an alternate authorized tool; after three use the smallest executable repair. After five fully completed rounds with no new evidence, **block but never mark complete** with a diagnostic explaining what is missing. A real human-authorized resume resets the fence. No new budget selector, reviewer, teammate or external side effect is introduced.

Model continuation and both shipped Kira personas explicitly discourage repeated inspections and large artifact transfer through chopped-up model text. Existing completion gates, explicit user cancellations, quota blockers, and parallel-team limits remain authoritative.

## Verification
Pure progress ledger regression: assistant-only rounds, novel read, repeated same tool/args, successful edit. Host integration regression: five consecutive assistant-only goal rounds persist a `no-progress` blocker instead of cycling. Runtime build and actual Windows task remain to be validated.
