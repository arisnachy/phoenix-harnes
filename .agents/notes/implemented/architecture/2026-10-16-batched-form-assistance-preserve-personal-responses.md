# Delegated forms: complete work in one pass without fabricating respondent testimony

## Incident
Brandpoll's 20-field "Unaided Associations" survey was loaded in Chrome after the microbrowser recovered, but Kira requested each field's spontaneous personal impression separately. The user answered "nada" for Beond only. Repeated refusal and single-field prompting created an execution loop; general model-visible instructions did not distinguish objective form data from unverifiable subjective attestation.

## Behavior
Inspect fields once, reuse the authenticated tab and fill authorized objective answers in bulk. On genuinely respondent-specific spontaneous associations or attestations, create one bundled review of remaining unknowns with optional clearly labeled suggestions, not 19 sequential questions; never treat generative text as the respondent's spontaneous opinion, and do not silently replicate one field's "nada" across all entries. A survey authorization does not itself attest subjective answers. Verify field state and submission receipt; otherwise report precise pending work.

Scope is universal to surveys/forms in the shared Goal prompt and Kira Max/standard personas. No browser security bypass or impersonation is introduced. The UI's embedded browser may remain limited; use a real connected browser session when available.
