logo, distinctive visual identity, product presentation, or other marks used to
identify the official PHOENIX project.

To the extent protected by applicable law, those PHOENIX source-identifying
marks and brand assets are reserved by Arisnachy Gómez Díaz and the PHOENIX
project. This policy does not claim that any mark is registered unless a
separate official notice expressly says so.

## Allowed without separate permission

You may:

- truthfully state that software is based on, compatible with, or derived from
  PHOENIX;
- link to the official repository;
- use the PHOENIX name in ordinary factual commentary, reviews, documentation,
  academic work, news reporting, and interoperability descriptions; and
- preserve required historical copyright and attribution notices.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, partnership,
or official status when none exists.

## Forks and modified distributions

A materially modified fork or independently operated distribution must not use
the PHOENIX name, logo, or confusingly similar branding as its own product
identity in a way likely to make users believe it is an official PHOENIX build.
Use a distinct product name and visual identity, while retaining factual
attribution required by the applicable software licenses.

## Commercial brand use

Using PHOENIX branding as the primary identity of a commercial product,
hosted service, reseller offering, conference sponsorship, merchandise, or
other source-identifying commercial offering requires separate written
permission from the PHOENIX project owner unless applicable law independently
permits the use.

their respective owners. PHOENIX uses those names only for truthful upstream
attribution and does not claim ownership of them.

For permission relating to PHOENIX marks, contact the repository owner through
